## PI-1: Steal a creature from La Silueta

- **Requested:** 2026-11-08, by the user.
- **Idea:** La Silueta keeps a creature of its own (for example a captive cría or a creature it guards in its lair), and the player can steal it from La Silueta itself.
- **Why parked:** the user asked to save it for later, after the stronger Silueta chase and the lethal island parents land.
- **Questions to settle before design:**
  - What La Silueta keeps, and where (inside its territory, at the Corazón de la Niebla, or carried).
  - How the theft starts: sneak in while it patrols, lure it away with noise, or a timed window at dawn.
  - What happens after the theft: a full-speed chase to camp, or a chase that ends only at a hide spot.
  - The reward: a unique species, a large Ember payout, or a collection milestone.
  - How it fits the fair-strike rules (`docs/mechanics/wildbound-fair-strike.md`) and the expedition economy floors.
- **Status:** parked. Do not build without an explicit request.
