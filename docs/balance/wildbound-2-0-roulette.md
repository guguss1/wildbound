## Notes for the user and T09b

- Pity Rara+ (default, 31% per spin): fires on 1.13% of paid spins, adds 0.277 Esencia of cash and 0.454 of assumed value per spin, and caps the drought at 9 (OFF: the seeded run reached 19). Money-loop caps hold: True.
- Pity Epica+ (the earlier chest design, 9.0% per spin): fires on 6.31% of paid spins, cash 24.96 and value 44.69 per spin. Money-loop caps hold: False (cash cap 22.50); validate() rejects it with today's caps, so choosing it means lowering the Epica/Legendaria resale (special eggs) or raising maxCashShare. Cash stays under the 45 price either way, so it is not a true loop.
- Conflict: the earlier base-chest design (docs/design/wildbound-hub-expansion.md section 6) said a paid draw never pays Esencia; the 2.0 pack weights give Esencia 35%. This table follows the 2.0 weights; the user should confirm.
- Saved state: draw() needs `pityCount` and `paidSpins` beside `freeSpinUsed` and `pending` in the planned roulette record (docs/design/wildbound-2-0-schema-plan.md).
- Glove recycle values (3/6/12/25) come from the hub-expansion design (Q8); no live module has them yet.
- A pending prize mints nothing until claimed (schema plan); a consumable at its cap converts to its fullEsencia.
